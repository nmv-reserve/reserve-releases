## Reserve v3.0.5

**What's new:**
- Local data is now encrypted at rest on your device, adding an extra layer of protection for your financial data.
- Backup and restore is more reliable, including restoring a backup onto a different computer.
- Fixed an issue where "Reset Financial Year" could fail with an error.
- Fixed an issue where a suspended company's settings could still be edited.
- License status messaging is clearer when a license has been revoked.

As always, back up your data (Settings → Backup) before updating.
